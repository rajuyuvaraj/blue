About blue

Streaming Notice: This site does not host, re-encode, or store any music files. Every song streams live directly from YouTube through YouTube's official player API.

Rights & Ownership: All rights to the music, compositions, and recordings belong entirely to the original artists, record labels, and YouTube. BLUE is an aesthetic interface for enjoying and reflecting on music that is already public on YouTube.

Playback Availability: If a particular song does not play, it is typically because the copyright owner has restricted embedding outside the YouTube domain. We respect these restrictions and automatically skip to the next available melody.

Reflections & Comments: Song reflections are written anonymously by listeners and do not represent the views of or endorsement by the original creators or artists.

Submit your playlist: Want your favorite playlist featured here? Send it to us at yuva0night@gmail.com and we might add it to BLUE.
